Our Story & Heritage

Crafting Unforgettable Flavors in the Heart of Dinajpur

Founded in 2024 by Ragib Hosen, Ragib's Kitchen is a modern premium restaurant and café located in Modern Mor, Dinajpur Sadar. We serve delicious food prepared with fresh local ingredients, quality products, and carefully crafted artisan recipes.
